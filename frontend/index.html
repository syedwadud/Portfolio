A little about me

I like to look closely, listen properly, and make something clear without making it flat.

I have spent the past eleven years working across media, research and communication. I come from a background in film and development studies, and I am usually drawn to work that asks for patience, care and a little imagination.

That has taken me from documentaries and animations to campaigns, knowledge platforms and editorial work—often around gender, public health, climate, water and sanitation.
